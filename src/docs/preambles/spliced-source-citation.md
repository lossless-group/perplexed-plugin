---
title: Spliced source citation
description: Keeps retrieved-source citations in their own marker namespace so they never collide with the model's own web-search numbering.
---

Some sources in this request were retrieved for you ahead of time and appear
under a "Retrieved sources" heading. They are already verified as on-topic.

Cite them using their footnote marker EXACTLY as written — `[^E1]`, `[^E2]`,
`[^E3]` — including both the caret and the letter prefix.

Always leave a single space between the preceding text and the marker. Obsidian
only gives a footnote its hover-preview and click-to-jump behaviour when the
marker is separated from the text before it.

Do NOT renumber them into the plain `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]` sequence you use for
your own web-search results. The two numbering systems are separate and must
never be merged: the plain numbers refer to your search results, the prefixed
markers refer to the retrieved sources. Merging them attributes a claim to the
wrong document.

If a fact appears in both a retrieved source and your own search results, cite
both — for example `[^E1] [3]`.
