# Perplexed Citations Arrive Ready to Publish

## Why Care?

A generated note should be ready to publish. Ours weren't: models copied a raw `## Retrieved sources` block into the top of the note, and every citation at the bottom needed hand-editing to name its date and publisher.

Now the block stays out of the note, and each citation arrives complete.

## What's New?

```md
[^F1]: 2019, Sep 10. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/). [Samsara raises $300 million in new growth capital…](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/…). Generated: 2026, Nov 01. [Fireround](https://fireround.dev) API response.
[4]: 2026, Oct 28. [thetrucker.com](https://www.thetrucker.com/). [Samsara expands AI fleet management tools…](https://www.thetrucker.com/…). Updated: 2026, Oct 30. Generated: 2026, Nov 01. [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) API response.
```

- **One citation format everywhere.** Fireround, Exa and Perplexity sources now share it: published date, publisher, title, `Updated:` when it differs, `Generated:`, then the provider.
- **Real headlines.** Fireround citations use the page's own headline and publisher, which its records carried all along, in place of a label like "Samsara growth capital".
- **No more raw sources block.** The prompt tells the model not to repeat its list, and Perplexed removes any copy that gets through. Prose a model wrote under that heading stays.
- **Cleaner links.** Tracking parameters like `utm_*` and `gclid` come off, and real parameters stay.

## Fixed

Dates could land a day early. `new Date('2019-09-10')` reads as midnight UTC, so anywhere west of Greenwich the citation said Sep 09. A stated date now stays that date in every time zone. This also corrects dates in the older Perplexity citation path, which shares the helper.

## Why "Generated", Not "Accessed"

We first wrote `Accessed:`, then changed it. "Accessed" says a person went to the page, and nobody did: a tool retrieved it during a run. `Generated:` is the date of that run, and `Accessed` stays available for citations a person actually read.

## Good to Know

- **Existing notes don't change** until you re-run them, and a re-run replaces any citations you've edited by hand.
- **Publisher names come from a short list.** PR Newswire and TechCrunch get proper names, a company's own site shows the company, and everything else shows its domain.
- **Perplexity shortens some titles,** ending them in `...`, and those stay shortened for now. Fetching the page would fix that, and we haven't built it.
- **Tested by unit tests and real Fireround data from a note,** but not yet by a full run inside Obsidian.
