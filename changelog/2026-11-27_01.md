# Real Book Covers, Given Room to Breathe

## Why Care?

The first thing you see on a source note is its cover. Until today, that cover was whatever an image search turned up for the book's title: sometimes the right one, sometimes a blog's photo of a stack of books, sometimes a LinkedIn banner. And it arrived jammed into a bullet, flush against the heading and the first line of prose.

Now a book's cover comes from the book's own record, and it gets a line of its own.

## What's New?

- **Covers from a proper source.** When a note has a `cover_image` (Metafetch's **Fetch book reference details for frontmatter** fills it from Open Library), that is the cover, whatever image search returned.
- **No cover beats a wrong cover.** A book note without a `cover_image` gets no cover at all, instead of a guess. Run the Metafetch command first and it will have one, when Open Library does.
- **Images stand on their own.** Any image on a line by itself is now a block: no bullet in front, a blank line above and below.

Before:

```markdown
# Chip War
- ![Cover of Chip War](https://i2.wp.com/someblog.com/chip.jpg)
- _A book about semiconductors and great-power rivalry._
```

After:

```markdown
# Chip War

![Cover of Chip War](https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/id/12968901-L.jpg)

- _A book about semiconductors and great-power rivalry._
```

## How it Works

The templates asked the model to put the image placeholder "on its own bullet". The model did, as `- [IMAGE 1: book cover]`, and the step that swaps markers for real embeds kept the bullet. The templates now ask for a line of its own, but a model doesn't always listen, so the fix lives in code: after the swap, every image-only line is cleaned up, whatever the model wrote.

The cover slot is the image between the first heading and the first `##` section. Images further down the entry (a diagram, an author photo) are left alone. So are notes that aren't books and have no `cover_image`: an organization keeps the logo image search found.

## What's Next

- **Existing notes.** This changes what new runs write. Notes generated before today keep their bulleted images and web-search covers until they're regenerated or fixed in a sweep.
